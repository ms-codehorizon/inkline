Theo, AI Startup Founder

Theo: Can we spend two minutes, eyes closed, meditating before we start?
Dana: Sure. Let's do that.
(Both close their eyes. Dana opens one eye, looks at him, closes it.)
Dana: That's a first.
Dana: Does meditation help you think?
Theo: No. The app is still deploying.
Theo: And this way I don't have to watch the logs.
Dana: Do you write code?
Theo: Barely.
Dana: Do you know how to debug it?
Theo: No. I copy the error and paste it back in.
Theo: I know nothing about software.
Dana: You must know something.
Theo: I know what I like and what I don't like.
Theo: And I'm very clear when I type, "No, not like that."
Dana: And you've launched three software companies this way?
(The phone on the table lights up and chimes.)
Theo: Four. One finished while we were meditating.
(Dana crosses out a number in her notebook.)
Theo: Nobody has opened it yet. Including me.
Dana: Do you have any tests?
Theo: I don't. The agent deleted them so the build would pass.
(Dana opens her mouth to speak, then gives up.)
Theo: If you start thinking about edge cases, you're not focused on shipping this beautiful demo.
Theo: It undermines the purity of the vibe.
(The laptop screen: a code diff scrolling far too fast to read. Theo's hand hovers over it. On screen: "4,000 lines of diff. Felt, not read.")
Dana: How many paying customers do you have?
Theo: Across all four companies?
Dana: Yes. In total.
(Long pause. Theo keeps smiling.)
Theo: Should we meditate again?
(Both close their eyes. The phone chimes.)
Theo: That's five.
(Dana opens one eye.)
Dana: Customers?
Theo: No, Companies.
(Theo keeps his eyes closed, satisfied. Dana slowly looks into the camera.)
(On screen: "No customers were harmed in the making of these companies." Then: "None were involved.")
