# Cast: founder_interview

Theo and Dana. Both are invented and must not resemble any real person. Approved look: `founder_interview-look/`.

## Theo - the guest
- **Look:** egg-shaped shaved head, short stubble (a few ink dots on the jaw), round glasses, collarless linen shirt with a button placket and rolled sleeves, dark trousers. Sits cross-legged and barefoot in a soft armchair.
- **Camera A:** close-up, sits left of centre, faces right.
- **Default face:** calm, eyes open, small closed-mouth smile.
- **Never:** laughs, smirks, looks at the camera, gets angry.
- **Voice:** per episode. The founder was warm, low, soft and slow; another guest can sound different, but stays calm and sincere.
- **Role:** a different person every episode, drawn the same way. The job comes from the script's title. His voice is set per video in `video.md` and may change; the look never does.

## Dana - the interviewer
- **Look:** shoulder-length curly hair drawn as loose loops, a few loops in soft ink for the silver streaks, blazer with clear lapels over a plain tee. Sits upright, notebook on her knee, pen in hand.
- **Camera B:** close-up, sits right of centre, faces left.
- **Default face:** neutral, attentive, mouth flat.
- **Never:** laughs, mocks him, looks at the camera.
- **Voice:** crisp, neutral, measured. Slower and quieter as the interview goes on.

## Shared vocabulary (what stages may ask for)
| Part | Values |
|---|---|
| eyes | `open`, `half`, `closed`, `one_open` (Dana), `up_side` (looking up and away), `camera` (into the lens; use once per video at most) |
| brows | `neutral`, `raised`, `knit` |
| mouth | `rest`, `smile`, `flat`, `open` (only for a silent "about to speak") |
| head | `level`, `tilt`, `lean` (forward), `down` (to notes or phone) |
| Theo hands | `knees`, `palm_up`, `finger_up`, `chop`, `paste`, `frame`, `heart`, `hover` |
| Dana hands | `pen_ready`, `writing`, `cross_out`, `notebook_flat`, `elbows_knees`, `gesture` |

## Voices
Set per video in `videos/<slug>/video.md`. Imported ElevenLabs clips or local text-to-speech; no cloning, no imitation of anyone.
